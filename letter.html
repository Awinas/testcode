A letter, just for you

Read it slowly. This page isn't going anywhere.

Dear Ishami,

Happy birthday to one of the sweetest souls I know. ♡

I hope this new chapter brings you the kind of happiness that stays, the kind of peace that feels like home, and little moments that remind you how loved you are. May it be full of fresh tulips, friendly cats, and sweet surprises around every corner.

May your days be soft, your dreams grow bigger, and may you one day own a bookshelf so full it needs a second one. May a cat choose your lap on the days you need comfort, and may tulips find their way to you just because.

Keep being your kind, lovely, wonderfully-you self. You don't have to become someone else to deserve good things. You already deserve them.

And whenever life gets a little heavy, remember: even the most well-loved stories have difficult chapters. That never means the story stops being beautiful. You matter more than you realise, and the world is better with you in it.

Here's to another year of pretty skies, silly laughs, unexpected adventures, and all the good things still finding their way to you. 🌷

May you always find a cat when you need comfort, a cozy blanket when you need escape, and a tulip when you need a reminder that lovely things can grow.

With love, always ♡
